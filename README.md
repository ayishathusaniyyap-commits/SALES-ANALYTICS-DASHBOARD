# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

**Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard created to analyze and visualize sales performance.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of important business metrics such as **Total Sales, Total Cost, Total Profit, Quantity, Orders, and Profit Margin**. It also includes interactive filters and charts for analyzing sales by month, product, payment mode, region, and category.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into an interactive and visually appealing dashboard that can help users:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze monthly sales and profit
- Identify product-wise sales performance
- Compare regional sales and profit
- Analyze sales by payment method
- Compare sales and profit by category
- Track key business KPIs

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis
Compares sales and profit across different months.

### 2. Payment Mode Sales Analysis
Shows sales distribution across different payment methods, including:

- Net Banking
- UPI
- Cash
- Card

### 3. Product-Wise Sales Analysis
Displays sales performance for products such as:

- Laptop
- Monitor
- Headset
- Keyboard
- Mouse

### 4. Regional Sales & Profit Analysis
Provides a comparison of sales and profit across different regions.

### 5. Monthly Sales Trend
Visualizes the contribution of different months to overall sales.

### 6. Sales & Profit by Category
Compares sales and profit across product categories such as:

- Electronics
- Accessories

## 🔎 Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters/slicers for:

- **Order Date**
- **Payment Mode**
- **Product**

These filters allow users to dynamically explore the sales data and update the dashboard visualizations.
